# Product Guidelines: Work Time Tracker

## 1. User Experience (UX) Principles
- **Clarity & Simplicity:** Ensure core actions (e.g., clocking in/out, requesting leave) are prominent and require minimal taps.
- **Feedback & Reassurance:** Provide immediate, clear feedback for all user actions, especially sensitive operations like attendance validation or password resets.
- **Consistency:** Maintain a unified visual language and interaction model across both the mobile and web platforms.

## 2. Prose Style & Tone
- **Professional & Helpful:** Use a respectful, clear, and concise tone appropriate for a workplace tool.
- **Direct Language:** Use active voice and straightforward instructions (e.g., "Submit Request" instead of "Click here to submit your request").
- **Localization Ready:** Keep text modular and culturally neutral to simplify future localization efforts (e.g., Ukrainian/English).

## 3. Visual & Branding Guidelines
- **Modern & Clean:** Utilize Material 3 guidelines on Android for a familiar, modern aesthetic.
- **Accessibility First:** Ensure high contrast ratios, readable font sizes, and clear visual hierarchy for all UI elements.
- **Responsive Design:** Interfaces must adapt fluidly to various screen sizes and orientations on both mobile and web.
